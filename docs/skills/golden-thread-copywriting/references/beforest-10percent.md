## Current journey and purpose

Multiple sources → guided walkthrough → Envision → trial stay → main product nurture.

For incomplete walkthrough visitors, the short reading page continues the same story and leads to Envision. Existing audiences can enter at the relevant point. We are helping a problem-aware reader understand a possible solution, then earn a trial request. Envision is a modular lead magnet and can be replaced; it is not the core product or a commercial entitlement calculator.

Core thread: **Give time in nature a place in your year.**

The page develops that thought through real land, familiarity from returning, recurring access, the reader's chosen places and people, an imagined calendar, and a trial experience. The final CTA should make the immediate step clear even when the wider objective is a trial request.

## Voice and shape

- Speak calmly and directly to the reader. Invite curiosity and longing through specific, ordinary moments.
- Keep Beforest's living landscapes central. Explain the land's significance in plain language.
- Use “you” naturally. Do not repeatedly tell the reader what they must feel.
- Prefer short paragraphs and brief headings. No em dashes, hype, luxury claims, generic wellness claims, or artificial scarcity.
- Use actual Beforest photographs and approved brand typography when implementation includes visual work. No green UI was requested for this approved page.
- Keep one primary CTA: “Envision your year”. The trial bridge explains what follows that step.

## Product facts must remain separate from imagination

- The committed direct-gemini walkthrough describes a 10% plan with **30 person-nights per year for 10 years**. Verify current terms before future use. Other offers may have different allowances.
- One person-night means one person staying one night. It is not a room-night or one full family's stay-night. Never abbreviate commercial allowances to generic “nights” when that obscures consumption.
- Envision displays an illustrative calendar of possible visits chosen by the visitor. It does not reserve dates, confirm access to every property, compute eligibility, or guarantee membership usage.
- Describe its inputs as people, reasons for time away, travel constraints, timing, rhythm, and places. These stated choices support useful personalisation. Keep them distinct from observed actions and classification.
- Trial stays are at **Blyton Bungalow, Coorg**. The paid booking journey supplies live dates, amounts, and stay terms. Never call the trial free.
- Harsha's rule: trial payment can be credited to an eligible 1cent subscription within **30 days after the trial stay is completed**. Confirm the offer's price, eligibility, and recorded completion before communicating a balance or expiry. Do not put stale prices in reusable copy.
- 10percent is access without land ownership. Do not imply collective ownership or guaranteed unrestricted access.

## Personalisation that continues the promise

Use selected motivation before generic labels. A person choosing family travel may want exploration as much as together-time. A solo visitor may want walking rather than quiet. Prefer “You chose time for walking” to “As a solo traveller, you need to recharge”.

After an unfinished calendar, offer a link and help with the next choice. After a viewed or saved calendar, use the selected landscape or rhythm to invite a trial. After a trial request, stop awareness reminders and move to main product nurture and actual booking support. A question should be answered before adding another nudge.
